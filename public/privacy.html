Privacy Policy

How Al-Khair Academy handles information shared through this website. Last updated October 2026.

Information we collect

When you send an admission enquiry, we collect the details you enter: parent or guardian name, student name, class sought, phone and WhatsApp numbers, preferred contact method and your message.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and guide you through admission.
  • To contact you by your preferred method about that enquiry.

We do not sell or share your information with others for marketing. Enquiry data is kept only as long as needed for admission purposes.

WhatsApp and third-party services

If you choose to send your enquiry through WhatsApp, the message is handled under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy. This website may use Google Analytics to understand visits in aggregate.

Photographs

Photos on this website show school events and activities. If you would like a photo of your child removed, please contact the school office and we will remove it.

Student records

This website does not hold student records, results or other private school data.
