FAQ

Questions parents ask

Which board does Al-Khair Academy follow?

Al-Khair Academy is a State Board school (Madhya Pradesh), and classes are taught in the CBSE pattern.

Is Al-Khair Academy a recognised school?

Yes. The school is recognised by the Zila Shiksha Kendra (District Education Centre), Indore under the RTE Act, 2009, for Pre-primary (Nursery) to Class 8. Recognition Code 82237, School ID PS82237, valid from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028. The school is run by Al Khair Educational And Social Welfare Society.

Which classes are offered?

Nursery to Class 8.

What is the age for Nursery admission?

Children aged 3 years and above are eligible for Nursery.

How is Nursery taught?

Our Nursery follows the Montessori method: children learn by doing, through hands-on materials, activities and role play.

Is there an entrance test or interview for admission?

No. In line with the RTE Act, 2009, there is no admission test and no screening of children or parents. After admission, our teachers get to know each child so they can support them from the first day.

Which documents are needed for admission?

Aadhaar Card, Samagra ID, Birth Certificate and Caste Certificate.

Is religious education included?

Yes. Through Understanding Quran, children learn the meaning of the Quran from a young age, along with hadith, duas and the sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ.

Does the school have smart classes?

Yes. Lessons use Propel audio-visual content on smart TVs in the classroom.

Is transport available?

Yes, a rickshaw transport facility is available. Please ask the office about routes.

How do I contact the school?

Call or WhatsApp +91 62646 72034, email alkhair.indore176@gmail.com, or send the enquiry form on this page.
