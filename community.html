Community

Learning Together in Khajrana

Khajrana is a diverse neighbourhood of Indore. Its schools, organisations and well-wishers work together so that every child has more chances to learn, compete and grow.

KES Khajrana Educational Society

Schools of Khajrana, together

The Khajrana Educational Society (KES) brings the schools of Khajrana together under one common society. Through KES, children from different schools meet in inter-school competitions, olympiads and sports.

Al-Khair students take part every year, and have brought home trophies from the KES Olympiad and inter-school events.

U Urooj

Guidance for our girls

Urooj is a non-profit organisation in Khajrana that supports students, mainly girls, with guidance and support for their education.

Its programmes, such as UROOJ 2026: Unfolding Stories of Success held in Indore, bring students together for recognition, mentorship and direction for the future.

★ Community & Well-wishers

A neighbourhood that celebrates together

Philanthropists and well-wishers of Khajrana support education in many ways, from encouraging students to bringing the community together for occasions such as Jashn-e-Azadi, our celebration of Independence.

Educational Visits

Learning from our city

Indore's heritage is our classroom too. Students visit places such as Rajwada and Lal Bagh Palace, where history and culture become real.
